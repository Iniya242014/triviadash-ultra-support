TriviaDash Ultra Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

TriviaDash Ultra is designed with privacy in mind. The current version of TriviaDash Ultra does not require users to create an account.

Photos and Custom Avatars

If you choose a photo for a custom avatar or other personalization feature, the photo is stored locally on your device. TriviaDash Ultra does not upload these photos to a server.

Audio Recordings

Audio recordings created or imported in TriviaDash Ultra are stored locally on your device. TriviaDash Ultra does not upload these recordings to a server.

Firebase

Online features such as Multiplayer and Global Leaderboard are currently marked as Coming Soon. Firebase connectivity for these features is disabled in the current version of the app.

Data Collection

TriviaDash Ultra does not collect personal data from users in the current version of the app.

Data Sales

TriviaDash Ultra does not sell personal information.

Future Features

This Privacy Policy may be updated if future versions of TriviaDash Ultra introduce online features or change how information is processed.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: triviadashultra@gmail.com
